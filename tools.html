Financial Tools

Three practical tools you can use right now: plan your monthly budget, estimate a savings timeline, and track your daily expenses.

How these tools work: everything runs inside your browser using JavaScript. Your numbers are never sent anywhere. The expense tracker saves your entries on this device only, using your browser's localStorage — so they remain after you close the page, but are not shared between devices.

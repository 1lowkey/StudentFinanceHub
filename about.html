About & Contact

Why Student Finance Hub exists, who it is for, and how to share your feedback with us.

Our background

Student Finance Hub was created to close a simple gap: many university students are never taught how to manage money, yet they are expected to do it well from day one on campus.

This website brings together clear explanations, realistic examples in Ghanaian cedis, and free interactive tools — all in one place, accessible on any phone or computer. It is an educational project, not a commercial service, and everything here is free to use.

Good to know: the tools run entirely in your browser and we do not collect your financial data.
🎯

Our objectives

  • Explain financial literacy in simple, practical language.
  • Provide free tools accessible on any device.
  • Use realistic Ghanaian examples with GH₵ amounts.
  • Encourage healthy money habits early in adult life.
  • Offer a way for students to give feedback.

Who this is for

The hub is designed primarily for tertiary students in Ghana, and also helps anyone managing money for the first time.

🎓

University & tertiary students

Students balancing allowances, hostel costs, and study expenses for the first time.

🧑‍💼

First-time earners

Recent graduates and part-time workers building their first personal budget.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parents & guardians

Anyone supporting a student and wanting practical money guidance to share.

Benefits to university students

What you gain by putting these lessons and tools to use.

1

Confidence with money

You understand where your money goes and how to plan for the month ahead.

2

Fewer money problems

An emergency fund and a budget reduce the chance of running out or borrowing.

3

Ability to reach goals

You can save deliberately for a laptop, a business, or further study.

4

Less stress

Knowing your finances are under control frees you to focus on your studies.
